Naa Ammadi ❤️

Nuvvu leni jeevitham naaku jeevitham kaadhu Ammadi…

Nuvvu naa pakkana unte nenu ee prapanchanni kuda jayinchagalanu…

Nuvvu naatho unte naaku emi kavali ani anipinchadhu… nuvve naa anni…

Nee navvu chusthe naa badha anni marchipotha…

Nuvvu maatladithe naa rojanta santhosham tho nimpothundi…

Nuvvu naatho unte nenu strongest person avutha…

Nee kosam nenu yedaina cheyadaniki ready Amadi…

Nuvvu naa jeevitham lo vachinappudu nenu complete ayyanu…

Nuvvu lekunda nenu okka nimisham kuda alochinchalekapotha…

Prathi roju ninnu marinta ekkuvaga preminchutunna…

Nee premanu pondadam naa adhrushtam…

Nuvvu naa prapancham… naa oopiri… naa santhosham… naa strength…

Nee peru vinagane naa gunde fast ga kottukuntundi…

Nuvvu naa life lo unte naku impossible ane word undadhu…

Ninnu preminchadam naa alavatu kaadhu… naa jeevitham…

Forever ninnu premisthune untanu Ammadi… ❤️
